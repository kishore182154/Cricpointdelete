How to request deletion

You can ask us to delete your CricPoint account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

  1. Send an email to support@example.com from the email address on your CricPoint account (or tap the button below).
  2. Use the subject Delete my CricPoint account and include your CricPoint username and the mobile number registered on the account.
  3. To protect your account, we may confirm the request by replying to your registered email or sending a one-time code to your registered mobile number.
  4. We delete the account within 30 days of confirming the request and email you when it's done.
Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account: username, full name, email address, mobile number and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Your profile photo.
  • Your entries on team player lists (squads), including your name and phone number.
  • Your saved app settings and favourite team.

This data is removed from our cloud database (Google Firebase). Data stored on your own phone is removed when you uninstall CricPoint.

What may be kept

  • Match scorecards recorded by other players or teams (runs, wickets and ball-by-ball records) are part of those teams' and tournaments' history. They are kept, but on request we replace your name in them with "Deleted player" — just say so in your email.
  • Sponsor ad view and tap counts are anonymous totals and are not linked to you.
  • Copies in our backups are overwritten within 90 days.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Delete only some of your data

If you want to keep your account but remove specific data — for example your profile photo or your mobile number from a team list — email support@example.com with your username and what you want removed. We handle these requests within 30 days.

Tip: you can change your profile photo, name and squad details yourself inside the app at any time.
